PixelSynfony Chat

PixelSynfony Chat is a small tool that posts occasional, friendly messages in the live chat of the @PixelSynfony YouTube channel: short reminders (drink water, stretch) and questions for the community.

It runs only for the channel owner, on the owner's own server, and posts only to the owner's own live stream. It does not read, collect or store viewers' chat messages or personal data.
